- Another nucleic acid exists alongisde DNA in the chromatin which possess a different sugar called ribose - being named ribose nucleic acdis or RNA
- RNA differs further from DNA in that it contains a base called urcail in place of thymine
- Biologists initially believed that RNA was only contained in plant cells whereas DNA was restricted to animal cells but this theory dissapated as improved chemical methods discovered nucleic acids in the cells of all life forms on Earth
- The primary structure of DNA and RNA is so similar because the former serves as a blueprint for the creation of a special kind of RNA molecule called mRNA. mRNA is created during RNA transcription - during which a strand of DNA is used as a tempalte for consturcting a strand of RNA by copying nucleotides one at a time. 
- In eukaryotes, DNA remmains in the nucleus while RNA can enter the far reaches of the cell to carry out DNA's instructions.
