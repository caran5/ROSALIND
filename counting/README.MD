Notes:
- A cell is considered to be the building block of life. 
- A nucleus was identified as the hub of cellular activity.
- Nuceli are filled with macromolecules called chromatin, a molecule that condenses into long thin, strings called chromosomes during mitosis. 
- Nucleic acids are a type of chromatin which can be polymerized into strands
- The nucleic acid monomer is called a nucleotide comprised of a sugar molecule, a nucleobase, and an a phosephate group. 
- A key point is that the nucleotide of a specific type of nucleic acid strand contain the same sugar and phosphate molecules, but differ in base.  Thus, one strand of a nucleic acid can be differentiated from another based solely on order of bases which define its primary structure.
